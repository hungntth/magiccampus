# Economy (M5): bank and bag pages, repair, shops, crafting, equipment upgrades, transport — Implementation Plan

**Date**: 2026-11-04 · **Status**: Approved (2026-11-04; the user pre-approved M5–M8 plans and asked for autonomous execution with one commit per milestone)
**Goal**: the player stores items in the bank and uses extra bag pages and the quest/pet-item pages, repairs gear, pays with the chosen currency and buys
in the mall, crafts equipment from materials, combines materials and gems, and upgrades equipment:
- stars;
- sockets and gems;
- element, bind, soul and prefix re-rolls;
- tier upgrade;
- disassembly;
- maker signature.

They can also teleport with transport scrolls. Today the bag has a single 30-slot page, the shop only buys and sells at NPCs, and equipment can only be worn.

Delivered in **five phases**, each implemented, lint/test-verified and browser-checked before the next:
- **A**: bank, pages, repair, sell password.
- **B**: payment and mall shops.
- **C**: crafting and mixing.
- **D**: equipment upgrades.
- **E**: transport and rename card.

Research base (the implementer reads both):
- `docs/research/2026-11-04_09_ECONOMY_CLIENT_RESEARCH.md`: Flash contract and decompile gaps (EquipFunc, BagPanel aborted), sid maps, bank flow, shops, crafting args, upgrade strings.
- `docs/research/2026-11-04_10_ECONOMY_SERVER_RESEARCH.md`: Go rules 1–52 tagged data-backed / Go-invented / stub, the cost table, push sequences, pseudocode.
- `docs/memory/inventory.md`, `shop.md`, `character-stats.md`, `pets.md`.

## 1. Confirmed mechanics and decisions

The research docs hold the rule details (with file:line). This section records the binding choices. *(H5)* marks a deliberate decision.

### 1.1 Phase A: bank, pages, repair
- Sid map (client and Go agree):
  - equip 1–22;
  - bank 301–450 (5 pages of 30);
  - bag 2101–2310 (7 pages);
  - quest bag 2311–2340 (slotType 4) and pet-item bag 2341–2370 (slotType 5).
  - Bank is a new slotType (use Go's number if it has one; otherwise `SlotType.BANK = 2`, and record it).
- `bagSlotNum` (default 1, max 7) and `bankSlotNum` (default 1, max 5) are character fields sent at login; they count opened pages.
- Page rules:
  - The quest page accepts item type 508 only; the pet-item page accepts kind 9 only; locked pages are refused with "Trang túi chưa mở.".
  - Moves use the existing `moveItem(srcSid, dstSid)` across pages.
  - `moveItemToPage(srcSid, pageType 1 bag | 2 bank, tab)` drops into the first free slot of that page ("Trang đã đầy." when full).
  - Shop buys, loot and quest rewards keep landing in the general bag pages (all open pages, not only page 1).
  - Quest items (508/509) keep going to the general bag (M2 decision), but the player may move them to the quest page.
- Bank:
  - `npcFuncClick` on an NPC of type 7 opens it; the password argument is ignored. Push `initViewBankPanel`; bank items come in `getInitSlot`.
  - *(H5)*: bank moves are allowed only while the bank is open (the session flag is set on open, cleared by `closeBank()`, on map change and on disconnect). Go had no gate.
  - `bankSort` is not registered (Go has none).
- Page books, *(H5 rule; Go never implemented them)*: using item 325 (bag book) adds +1 `bagSlotNum` (max 7) and item 324 (bank book) adds +1 `bankSlotNum` (max 5).
  Both are consumed. Push `onUPP {bagSlotNum|bankSlotNum}`; refused at max.
- Repair:
  - `repair(itemId, mode)` and `repairAll(mode)`; cost `(maxDurability - durability) * 10` money (Go-invented, adopted), paid in unbound money only.
  - `repairAll` repairs the equipped rows. Pushes `onAddCharactorSlot` per repaired item, then `onUPP{money}`.
  - *(H5)*: durability still never decreases (Go never called `ReduceDurability`); repair stays for parity and for items that start damaged. Gear wear is out of scope.
- `sellItem(itemId[, pass])`: the optional password argument is accepted and ignored (colour > 2 needed it in Flash).

### 1.2 Phase B: payment and shops
- `changeMoneyType(type)`: 1/2 silver (moneyBind / money), 3/4 gold (goldBind / gold). Reply `{flag, num}`.
  It persists `selectedMoneyType` / `selectedGoldType`, which the M1 shop already reads. Client payment radios go in the shop and bag panels.
- `unlockMoney(md5)` always returns `true` *(H5: no money lock / secondary password)*. The client never shows the lock.
- Mall / system shop:
  - `buySystemItemClient(slotId, n)` and `buySystemItemMulti` (implementer: the shape per the research; skip it if unconfirmed).
  - Slots come from `data_tbl_shop_slot` of the system shop ids (`SYSTEM_SHOP_ID`, tab = slot `st`).
  - Payment is gold (bind first per the selected gold type) or points. *(H5)*: point currencies are refused (no points system).
  - The pushes are the same as the NPC buy. A UI "Cửa hàng" (mall) panel opens from the HUD mall button.
- `getShopConfig` / `getLimitShopConfig` / `getRemainShopConfig` return the empty legacy shapes. The `amount` stock is not enforced (Go).
- VIP shop: deferred to M8 together with VIP levels. Credit and exchange shops, limit-time shops and mystery exchange are skipped (no Go logic, or event currencies).

### 1.3 Phase C: crafting and mixing
- Recipes are equipment templates with `makable == 1` and `require_item1..3` / `require_num1..3`.
- `getMakeColor(materials, equipTplId)` (preview) and `newMake(materials, equipTplId)` (awaited, reply `{flag}`), with `materials = {1..3: {tempBagFlag, idx}}` referencing bag items.
  - Rates from the material ColorCode levels (all levels 2–6 → the fixed table), otherwise `data_tbl_recipe_plan` (absolute percents; the remainder is failure).
  - Materials are consumed even on failure. No money cost.
  - The result: quality = bucket*5 + prefix, main lines re-rolled with the `EQUIPT_QUALITY` multipliers, 1–3 pre-drilled holes (40/35/25 %), a random element, the maker's name. All per server research §4.
- `materialMixOne` / `materialMixAll(num 2..5, itemRef, tempBagFlag)` → `{slotId, num, flag, finalNum}`.
  - Rate `num * (ColorCode == 4 ? 6 : 20)` %; success → ColorCode + 1 (cap 5).
  - *(H5)*: Go's rate is used; the client text for level 6 says 30 %. The mismatch is recorded.
- `jewelUpdateOne` / `jewelUpdateAll`: gem tier upgrade, rate `num * 20` %, following the gem chains.
- `newCook` / `newMedicine` (life skills) are deferred to M8.
- Equipment instances gain `properties`:
  - `quality`, `prefix`, `element`, `makerName`;
  - main and sub lines (`mainProp1/2`, `mainPropNum1/2`, `prop1/2`, `propNum1/2`);
  - `holeNum`, `t1..t10` (gems), `star`.
  - M1 stats read the template values unless the instance overrides them (`equip-stats.ts` already has the hook).

### 1.4 Phase D: equipment upgrades
- Star: `starOne(itemId, ...)` / `starAll` / `getStarNum` per the research. It consumes stone item 5; no money.
  - *(H5)*: the success-rate table uses the **client display index** (`star+1`): 0→1 100, 100, 90, 80, 70, 60, 50, 30, 30, 30.
  - *(H5)*: failure keeps the star (Go's "reset to 0 at ≥ 8" was invented).
  - The star multiplier table `[1, 1.1, 1.21, ...]` applies to the main lines in stats.
- Holes: `holeDig(itemId, slot)` needs a bound item and diamond (template 16) with ColorCode ≥ `floor((slot-1)/2)`. Cost 2000 silver by the selected money type; max 10 holes.
- Gems:
  - `jewelSet(itemId, hole, gemItemId)` / `jewelDel(itemId, hole)` / `getJewelData` / `getHoleNum`. The remove fee is 20 % of the gem's gold value (Go-invented, adopted), and the same gem returns to the bag.
  - *(H5)*: gem props map to stats with the shared equipment prop dictionary (`EQUIP_PROP`; prop 3 = maxSp per that dictionary). Go mapped only 1–5 and prop 3 to max HP; the dictionary is the more complete source.
    The implementer records the per-chain prop list.
- Element, bind, soul, prefix, maker name (two-step):
  - `changeElement` / `changeBind` / `changeSoul` / `changePrefix` / `changeName` preview the roll; each charges silver and consumes materials **at the preview** (Go). There is no refund on reject.
  - `sureChange*` applies the stored preview, which is kept in memory per character (lost on disconnect).
  - Costs: element 30000 + `require_item1`; bind 30000 + `require_item2`; soul 50000 × mode + items 1659–1662 by level band; prefix `req_level² × 10` + three template materials; maker name 10000 + `require_item3`, which needs a bound item of colour ≥ 4.
- `bindItem(itemId)`: binds an unbound item (Flash `Slot.as:725`).
- `changeLevel(itemId)` (tier upgrade): cost `next.req_level² × 10`. It creates the next-tier item in the same slot, resetting stars, holes and gems.
- `equResolve(itemId)` (disassembly): returns bound template materials, colour − 1 unless prefix 5.
- UI: a "Lò rèn" forge panel with tabs Ghép (mix), Chế tạo (craft), Cường hóa (star), Khảm (sockets/gems), Tinh luyện (element/bind/soul/prefix/name), Thăng cấp (tier), Phân giải (resolve).
  It opens from an NPC function or a HUD button; the implementer checks which HUD/system-bar button is "Chế tạo" (the HUD shows one).

### 1.5 Phase E: transport and rename
- `useTransport(itemId, mapId?)` per the client research: consumes a transport scroll and teleports through the existing scene transport.
  Without a scroll, `useItemGold({tid, mid})` pays 3 gold (normal) or 5 gold (senior / party leader). Followers move with the leader (M3).
- `changeNameByCard(newName)`: consumes item 2126 and renames the character (unique name check, client validation rules).
  Pushes `onUPP{name}` and a scene re-broadcast so others see the new name.
- Runes, temp bags (`getTBag` and friends: Go index arithmetic is inconsistent), the reward temp bag, `useRedBag` / `useRadar` / `useSeek` / `useTrack`: deferred.

## Implementation notes (phase A)

Implemented 2026-11-04. Baseline before: shared 25, swf 4, server 299. After: shared 30, swf 4, server 316; lint green.

- **Shared**: new `packages/shared/src/economy.ts` (+ `economy.test.ts`): `sidOf(slotType, slotIndex)` / `slotOf(sid)`, page constants, `PageType`, `BagTab`,
  `bagCapacity` / `bankCapacity` / `openBagPages` / `slotIsOpen`, `repairCost`, `RepairReply`, page-book ids 324 / 325. `SlotType` gained `BANK = 2`
  (Go's number), `QUEST_BAG = 4`, `PET_BAG = 5`. `PlayerData` and `PlayerUpdate` gained `bagSlotNum` / `bankSlotNum`.
- **Server**:
  - Capacity is now per character: `InventoryService.capacity(characterId)` is async and `add` / `canFit` / `canFitAll` use all opened general pages.
    Equip / unequip, pet gear return and trade swap use it too (`planSwap` takes an optional per-side `capacity`). Shop buy, loot and quest rewards
    already went through `add` / `canFit`, so they follow automatically.
  - `moveItem` / `moveItemNum` / `moveItemToPage` / `dropItem` work over bag, bank, quest and pet-item sids. The planners in `bag-rules.ts` are reused
    over rows keyed by sid. New files: `bag-pages.ts` (pure rules), `page-gate.service.ts`, `page-ops.service.ts`, `slot-writes.ts`.
  - Bank: `npcFuncClick` on an NPC of type 7 sets `session.bankOpen` and pushes `initViewBankPanel`. `closeBank` clears the flag; so do map join / leave.

  - Page books (324 / 325) are handled in `UseItemService.useConsumable` (`$max` write after the book is consumed, push `onUPP`).
  - `repair` / `repairAll` (`repair.service.ts`, `repair.rpc.ts`) live in `InventoryModule`, not the shop module as the plan table suggests.
    They charge the `money` field with a conditional decrement on the Character model, because `CharacterService` cannot be imported from `InventoryModule`
    (module cycle through `PetModule`).
- **Deviations / decisions**:
  - Merge across storages is allowed (Go refuses to stack rows of different slot types). A swap re-checks the page rule for the displaced item too.
  - Quest items on the quest page still count for quest "collect" progress and turn-in: `countByTemplate` / `removeByTemplate` read bag + quest page.
  - Pet gear sitting on the pet-item page can be equipped on a pet (`pet-equip.service.ts` accepts slotType 5 sources).
  - Bag tabs are `1..7 N.vụ Pet` (no `<` `>` arrows); tabs of unopened pages are disabled. Bag double-click deposits into the bank while the bank panel is open
    (Flash `bagToBank`), so items cannot be used by double-click until it is closed.
  - `dropItem` accepts bag, quest-page and pet-page rows; bank rows must be moved out first.
  - `sellItem` already ignored its second argument; no code change was needed. `bankSort` is not registered.
  - The bank window has no distance auto-close (the dialog and shop keep theirs); it closes on scene change and through its close button.
  - Shop "Sửa" arms the bag repair tool with mode 2; "Sửa hết" asks for a confirm and sends `repairAll(2)`; "Sửa hết (pet)" stays a placeholder.
    The bag toolbar Sửa chữa works as a click-an-item mode (mode 1). Repairing equipped gear is through repairAll only.
- **Verified in the browser** (test character, map 1, bank NPC 247): book 325 opens page 2 and enables its tab; items moved by tab drop and by slot drag;
  quest page accepts a type 508 item and refuses a potion / equipment; pet-item page accepts pet gear and refuses a sword; repair tool charged 700 silver;
  bank panel opened by `npcFuncClick(247)`, deposit by drag and by double-click, book 324 opens bank page 2, tab drop, withdraw by double-click, moves refused after
  `closeBank`; shop buy with a full page 1 lands in page 2 slot 0; shop "Sửa hết" repaired worn gear for 500 silver.
