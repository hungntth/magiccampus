# Pets (M4): pet core, map follow, pets in battle, catch, skills and equipment — Implementation Plan

**Date**: 2026-11-04 · **Status**: Approved (2026-11-04, including the H5 decisions marked in §1)
**Goal**: players obtain pets:
- from quest rewards (quest 4 "Nhận Pet" → Tiểu Nấm Yêu);
- by catching creatures in battle.

They manage pets in a Pet panel: battle pet, rest, rename, release, bind, attribute points, life restore. One pet can follow them on the
map, visible to others. The battle pet fights next to its owner (its own command each round, its own exp and life drain). Pets learn skills from books,
open more skill slots and wear pet equipment. Today, pets do not exist: quest pet awards are logged and skipped, and the HUD PetCanvas is empty.

Delivered in **five phases**, each implemented, lint/test-verified and browser-checked before the next:
- **A**: pet core and the panel.
- **B**: map follow.
- **C**: pets in battle.
- **D**: catch and pet quest objectives.
- **E**: skills, equipment, extra slots.

Research base (the implementer reads both):
- `docs/research/2026-11-04_07_PET_CLIENT_RESEARCH.md`: Flash contract.
  - data model and the wrapper `{id, data}`, `PET_LEVEL_EXP`, panel RPCs/pushes and gates, follow (`PetView`, `onCreatePet`/`onCancelPet`, `showPetObj`);
  - battle (cList type 39, 2-command batch, PetCanvas), catch (`ST_BATTLE_CATCH`, strings), skills and equipment UI, H5 delta.
- `docs/research/2026-11-04_08_PET_SERVER_RESEARCH.md`: Go rules.
  - persistence (§1), stat formula with growth scale plus test vectors (§2, §11), acquisition and catch formula (§3), management table (§4);

- `docs/memory/combat.md`, `party.md`, `character-stats.md`, `quest.md`, `inventory.md`, `main-hud.md`.

## 1. Confirmed mechanics

*(H5)* marks a deliberate deviation or a gap we fill.

### 1.1 Data and stats (phase A)
- Pet document (H5 `pets` collection; field names follow the Go/Flash keys):
  - identity: `petId` (counter `pets`), `characterId`, `tid` (creature template), `petName`;
  - progress: `level`, `exp` (cumulative, as the client expects);
  - growth: `growRate`, `growRateAdd`, `aptEx {strength, agility, stamina, intelligence, energy}`, `attrPoints`;
  - vitals: `life` (0..10000), `currentHp`, `currentMp`;
  - flags: `state` (1 battle, 3 rest; 2 unused), `binded` (0/1), `following` (bool);
  - `skills: number[15]` (-1 locked, 0 empty, > 0 skill id), `equ: number[8]` (item ids or 0);
  - `upgradeNum: 0`, `element: 0` (no growth yet).
- Stats: the character `buildBaseStats` with these inputs:
  - attribute = creature `att_X + (level-1) + aptEx.X + gear attr`;
  - per-mille apt = creature `apt_*`;
  - **growth scale `growRate + growRateAdd` instead of 0.8**;
  - seeds HP 20 / MP 5 / hit 100 / speed 100, SP 150, no creature `prop_*`.
  - Then gear flats → gear percents (props 24/25) → passive-skill buffs (phase E).
  - Test vectors for creature 32 (server research §11): Lv1 grow 1.6 → hp 972, atk 62, def 304; grow 0.8 → hp 496, atk 31, def 151; Lv10 columns.
- Exp: `PET_LEVEL_EXP` (cumulative). The implementer copies it from the Flash `GamePredef.as:8838+` and diffs it against Go `pet.go:857-875`; on a mismatch, Flash wins and the diff is recorded.
  - Level = largest L with `exp >= table[L-1]`; cap 160; **no owner-level cap** (Go).
  - +5 `attrPoints` per level gained. The base attributes do not change.
- `petMaxNum` per character, default 6 (Go), carried in `PlayerData`.
- Current HP/MP: clamped to max. *(H5)*: the battle pet starts every battle at full HP/MP (Go never persisted pet HP).
- Life: new pets 10000. *(H5)*: the life drain is applied at battle settle (phase C).

### 1.2 Acquisition (phase A for quests, phase D for catch)
- Creation (server research §3):
  - Capacity check `count + 1 <= petMaxNum`.
  - Defaults: name = creature name, level 1, `exp = 1` (cumulative start; table[0] = 1), `growRate = creature.grow_base` unless overridden, life 10000, state 3, attrPoints 0.
  - Skills: slots 1..5 = 0, seeded from `data_tbl_creature_skill(cid)` ordered by position, id; slots 6..15 = -1.
  - Unbound unless the source binds.
- Quest award kind 2 / type 12: `growRate = q/10`, bound. Pushed as `onAddPet({id, data})` after the item awards.
  - A full pet bag skips the pet and the quest still completes (Go). *(H5)*: also send a red note "Bạn không có đủ chỗ trống trong túi pet!".
  - The active pet getting the full quest exp (Go `service.go:1389`) is included in phase C, when pet exp exists.
  - Quest 4 client flow: the client opens the panel and calls `changePetState(petId, 1)` 500 ms after the finish; `onAddPet` must arrive before that.

### 1.3 Management (phase A)
All argument shapes follow the Flash call sites (client research), not Go where they differ.
- `initViewPetMngP()` → `{[id]: {id, data}}` (all pets).
  - `data` is the pet view: all keys of §1.1 plus `property` (`final*` + `lastPoint`, `growRate`, `growRateAdd`), `skill1..15`, `equ1..8`, `colorCode`, `resCode`, `iconCode`.
  - Login `PlayerData` gains `petMaxNum`, `showPetId`, `petList` (unwrapped `data` keyed by id), `showPetObj?`.
- `changePetState(petId, 1|3)`:
  - State 1 requires life > 0 (*(H5)*: refuse with a note; Go returned 500), demotes any other state-1 pet to 3, and sets `binded = 1`.
  - Pushes `onUpdatePet(id, "state", n)` for each changed pet (+ `"binded"`), then `onRefreshPetProp(id, property)`.
  - *(H5)*: server-side gates matching the client alerts: pet level − 5 ≤ owner level, and template `use_lv ≤ owner level`.
- `changePetName(petId, name)`: 1..20 chars; `""` resets to the template name. Push `onUpdatePet(id, "petName", name)`.
- `delPetByClient(petId[, pw])`:
  - *(H5)*: the battle pet cannot be released (Go had no gate). Equipped pet items go back to the bag; the following pet is cancelled.
  - Pushes `onDelPet(petId)` (+ `onCancelPet` if it was following). The password arg is ignored.
- `bindedPetByPlayer(petId)` → `onUpdatePet(id, "binded", 1)`.
- `changePetProperty(petId, {addStrength, addAgility, addStamina, addIntelligence, addEnergy})`: the same parsing and all-or-nothing rule as the character `changeProperty`.
  Pushes `onUpdatePet` per changed key (`attLastPoint`, `apt*Ex`), then `onRefreshPetProp`.
- Life restore: `useItem(2, petId, itemId)` on template type 507 → life 10000, `onUpdatePet(id, "life", 10000)`.
  *(H5)*: refused when life is already full; consumed through the existing use-item spend-then-credit path.
- `onUpdatePet(petId, key, value)` is positional; values are numbers except `petName`. `onRefreshPetProp(petId, property)` replaces `property` wholesale.
  The implementer confirms positional vs `{id, s}` against the client research open question and follows the client consumer.

### 1.4 Map follow (phase B)
- `startPetFollow(petId, currentShowPetId)` → reply truthy; it sets `following` (only one pet), and does not change `state`.
  `cancelPetFollow(showPetId)` → truthy. The client locks the button for 5 s.
- The map broadcast includes the owner: `onCancelPet(ownerCid)`, then `onCreatePet({cid, petData})` with `petData` = scene view (server research §10 keys; at least `id, cid, tid, name, level, resCode, colorCode, iconCode`).
- Scene payloads (`createChars`, `onScenePlayerEntered`, `sceneLogin` self) carry `showPetId` / `showPetObj` for players with a following pet. Non-AFK followers of a party (M3) do not show a pet (Flash).
- *(H5)*: the following pet is restored after relog (`showPetId` is sent at login; Flash lost it). The client's `cancelPetFollow` on map entry (the buff 1709/1979 rule) is **not** ported: pets keep following across maps.
- Client `PetView`: follows the owner when the distance is > 100 px, spawns behind the owner by facing, uses the creature sheet (`resCode`), is client-simulated, and has no movement pushes.

### 1.5 Pets in battle (phase C)
- The fighting pet is the owner's state-1 pet with life > 0 (Go also fell back to the following pet; *(H5)*: state 1 only, which is clearer). It sits at owner slot + 5 (15..19), in solo and group battles.
- Before `onSetCharState`, push `onUpdatePet(petId, "currentHp"/"currentMp", max)`.
  - cList entry: `{type: 39, id: petId, cid: ownerCid, tid, name, level, battleId, hp, hpMax, mp, mpMax, sp, spMax, resCode, colorCode, iconCode, element, bossFlag: 0}`.
- Commands:
  - `battleUpdateCmd([ownerCmd, petCmd?])`: index 0 is the owner, index 1 the pet. Readiness waits only for living player characters.
  - A missing pet command → basic attack on a random living enemy (Go: front line).
  - A pet skill command uses the pet's MP and must be a skill in the pet's slots. *(H5)*: ownership is checked; Go did not check.
  - A dead owner with a live pet: the client auto-defends the owner, and the server accepts the pet command.
  - `-70` catch and `-60` item are valid pet actions only if the Flash menu offers them; the menu has attack, skill, item, defence, escape, position, auto. In M4 the pet menu is attack / skill / defence.
- The victory check counts pets as side participants (legacy). Enemy AI may target pets.
- Settle:
  - On a win, pet exp = `floor(splitExp * 0.5)` per surviving owner's pet, even if the pet died (Go). Level-ups give +5 points.
  - Life −10 for every pet participant, any outcome.
  - Pushes at `battlePlayEnd`, per owner: `onUpdatePet(id, "exp", cumulative)`, `onUpdatePet(id, "life", n)`, `"level"` when it leveled, then `onRefreshPetProp`.
  - Pet HP is not persisted.
- Client:
  - Pet units at 15..19 face like their owner; the local player's pet gets the second command step: after the player's command the pet menu appears, then both are sent.
  - PetCanvas HUD (icon, name, level, HP/MP bars) shows the battle pet.
- `-90` recall / `-100` swap are not ported (the Flash bag-panel args are unknown).

### 1.6 Catch and pet objectives (phase D)
- Client: menu "Bắt" → `ST_BATTLE_CATCH` → click an enemy → `battleUpdateCmd([{tid, action: -70, id: 0, level: -1}])`.
  - "Pet đã đầy!" when pets ≥ `petMaxNum`.
  - "Pet cao hơn bạn 5 cấp, không thể bắt!" when `target.level − 5 ≥ player.level`.
- Server (server research §3 catch):
  - target is a creature with `catchable == 1` (Go parity; *(H5)*: the implementer counts the `catchable` value distribution in Mongo and records it in the plan notes);
  - level ≤ owner + 5; capacity free;
  - `rate = clamp(20 + round((1 - hp/hpMax)*65) + d, 5, 95)`, roll `rand(100) < rate`; no item.
- Success: the pet is created (grow_base, unbound, state 3); the target dies with behavior 10160 and counts as killed (exp, loot, quest credit).
  `onAddPet` is pushed at `battlePlayEnd` after the other pushes.
- Failure: a miss step with `sSObj.catchF = {cid, rate, selfCatch}`; the client shows the failure text from the research strings.
- Pet turn-in objectives (`quest_require` kind 3 / type 12):
  - Matching pets have the same template, an unchanged name, and growth colour ≥ `q/10` (bands `[1.1, 1.3, 1.6, 2.0, 2.5]`).
  - `isSupported` accepts them. `finishQuest` deletes the lowest-growRate matching pets (`onDelPet`). The client tracker counts pets.

### 1.7 Skills, equipment, slots (phase E)
- `petOpenSkill(petId, slot)`: slot 6..15 with value -1.
  - Cost `[10,20,40,70,110,160,220,290,370,460][slot-6] * {1:1, 2:3, 3:10}[qLevel]`, paid in gold, goldBind first.
  - Pushes `onUpdatePet(id, "skillN", 0)` + the money push.
- `petBook(petId, bookItemId)`, reply `{f, rf?, n?}` (server research §6): book type 506 / use_type 2; skill by name match; `cre_kind` gate; first empty slot; no conflict.
  - Success: `onUpdatePet(id, "skillN", sid)`, `onRefreshPetProp`; the book is consumed.
- `petDelSkill(petId, slot[, pw])`: the pet must be bound; consumes item 1364; the password is ignored. Pushes the slot push, `onUpdatePet(id, "skillN", 0)`, `onRefreshPetProp`.
- Passive skills (`type 11` with `buff_id`) add to the pet stats (server research §6). Active pet skills cast in battle through the existing skill-cast rules (base 10, mult 1.0, MP from the pet).
- Pet equipment (positions 50..57 → `equ1..8`):
  - Gates: `req_level ≤ pet level`, `req_class` ∋ creature `class_ids`, `req_class_id` ∋ pet `tid`.
  - `petEquipOn(petId, itemId)`: the displaced item goes to the freed bag slot. `petEquipOff(petId, itemId)` goes to the first free bag slot.
  - Pushes per server research §7, in the positional form the client consumers read (`onPetEquipOn(petId, pos, oldItemId, newItemId)`, `onPetEquipOff(petId, pos, itemId)`), plus the bag slot pushes and `onRefreshPetProp`.
  - Pet gear stats per §7. Equipped pet items leave the bag, in a new slotType 6 (`SlotType.PET_EQUIP`).
- `petOpenSlot()`: +1 `petMaxNum` (cap 180) for item 326's `gold` (20), goldBind first. Pushes the money push and `onUPP {petMaxNum}`.
- The client also gates pet equipment and books on bag double-click with the pet panel open (Flash: pet-equipment items double-clicked into `petEquipOn`).

## Implementation notes (phase A, 2026-11-04)
Built: pet data, stats, levels, quest award (kind 2), management RPCs, login payload, Pet panel and HUD PetCanvas. Phases B-E untouched.

- **PET_LEVEL_EXP diff**: Flash `GamePredef.as:8838+` (`PET_LEVEL_EXP[0..159]`) and Go `pet.go:857-875` are **identical** (160 entries, 1 .. 94838109102), diffed value by value. Copied into shared `pet.ts`. Helpers: `petLevelOfExp`, `petExpOfLevel`, `petGrowColor` (Flash `colorByGrowRate`).
- **Stats**: `buildBaseStats(profile, scale = CHARACTER_GROWTH_SCALE = 0.8)`; the character path is unchanged (all existing tests green). `modules/pet/pet-stats.ts` passes `growRate + growRateAdd`. The §11 vectors (creature 32, Lv1/Lv10, grow 1.6/0.8, all ten stats) are asserted in `pet-stats.test.ts` and pass; the browser panel for the quest pet shows 972/201/62/52/304/146/103/2/2/135.
- **Deviations from the plan text**:
  - Creature columns live in a separate `PetTemplate` (`gamedata/pet-data.ts`, `GameDataService.petTemplate(id)` / `creatureSkillIds(cid)`) instead of widening `CreatureTemplate`, so the combat tests and fighters stay untouched. The skill table is cached from `data_tbl_creature_skill` (`cid, sid, position`; the seed has `position null`, so rows order by id).
  - Pet `_id` is the pet id (counter `pets`) instead of a separate unique `petId` field. `currentHp/currentMp` are not stored: the view sends them at max (H5: pets never persist HP).
  - Pet exp is stored cumulative (starts at 1), level is stored too and always `petLevelOfExp(exp)`.
  - `PlayerData extends PetLoginInfo` (`petMaxNum`, `showPetId` = -1 when none, `petList` of unwrapped data blocks, `showPetObj?` reserved for B). `PetView` additionally carries creature facts the Flash client reads from TBL_CREATURE: `creatureName, classId, qLevel, useLv, resCode, iconCode, colorCode`, plus `level`, `characterId`, `following`.
  - Pushes use the positional form: `onUpdatePet(petId, key, value)`, `onRefreshPetProp(petId, property)`, `onDelPet(petId)`, `onAddPet({id, data})`. `changePetProperty` pushes `attLastPoint` and the five `apt*Ex` keys, then `onRefreshPetProp`.
