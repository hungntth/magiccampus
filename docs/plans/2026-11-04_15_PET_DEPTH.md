# Pet depth (M12): growth, evolution and contract, guard / talent / stone / soul gem, pet gear upgrades, PPVE tower, pet arena — Implementation Plan

**Date**: 2026-11-04 · **Status**: Draft
**Goal**: give the M4 pets (`docs/memory/pets.md`) the legacy growth and end-game systems:
- **Luyện pet**: stars, fusion, Tẩy Luyện / Phục Sinh / Tẩy Điểm, Ảo Hóa, element change, skill upgrade.
- **Tiến hóa window**: evolution, Vũ Hóa and Khế Ước, plus the Sách Tiến Hóa handbook.
- **Character-wide pet boosters**: Chiến Thú Hộ Vệ, Thiên Phú Pet, Tụ Linh Bảo Thạch and Mệnh Hồn.
- **Pet gear upgrades**: Thăng cấp, Đổi màu, Đổi khóa, Tái thiết lập, Thăng hoa, Đổi khắc chế and repair.
- **Pet battles**: the Không Gian Điêu Khắc tower (PPVE) and Đấu pet (pet arena).

Today, H5 pets have level, points, skills (books), gear, follow and battle. Everything above is a missing RPC; the legacy client calls each one
by name.

## 2. Confirmed mechanics

*(H5)* marks a deliberate deviation. Line refs are legacy Go unless marked Flash.

### 2.0 Cross-cutting
- **Owner level cap** (`domain/pet/owner_level_cap.go`; Flash `AUTOEXPPANEL_S[5]` "Pet đã vượt quá bạn 5 cấp, không thể tiếp tục nhận kinh nghiệm!", Language.as:912).
  - A pet can never pass owner level + 5 (max 160). Exp is clamped to the start of the cap level.
  - A pet already above the cap keeps its level and gains nothing.
  - H5 pets.md says "no owner-level cap (Go)"; that predates the Go fix of 2026-10-29. *(H5)*: adopt the cap in Phase A for the battle share, the quest exp and grow potions.
- **Pet reply/notice style**:
  - Refusals send a reason as `onSystemMidMsgOrNote` (or `onSystemSay` where noted) and a falsy or `{f:false}` reply. Nothing is spent on a refusal.
  - Materials are consumed before the save, with a rollback on a failed save (the H5 "spend, then credit" pattern).
- **Bound pet rule**: most Luyện pet ops need `binded = 1`, and a bound material binds the result (`PETFUNCPANEL_S[40]` "Bạn đã đặt vào pet đã khóa, pet sau khi dung hợp cũng sẽ khóa!").
- **Grow colour bands** `PET_GROWRATE_NUM [1.1,1.3,1.6,2.0,2.5]` (GamePredef:1459). Color = band index of `growRate`; shared `petGrowColor` exists.
- **Busy pet** = battle state 1, following, in a guard slot, in an arena/PPVE line-up, or wearing gear. Busy pets cannot be fusion/eat material.

### 2.1 Luyện pet (12.1a, `PetFuncPanel` tabs 0..7)
- **Cường hóa `petStar(petId, starItemId, safeItemId|null)`** (`application/pet/star.go`):
  - Star item: type 507 `prop_type` 3 (nội đơn). Success rate for the next star: `PET_STAR_SUCCESS [100,100,100,90,80,70,60,50,30,30,30,30,20]` (GamePredef:1456). Max 12 (`PET_STAR_MAX`).
  - Success: +1 star; `growRateAdd += propl_num/100`, plus 0.1 more on the successes that reach stars 9..12 (owner rule; 12 × Huyền Thú 0.13 = 1.96). The share is tracked in `starGrowAdd`.
  - Failure at 0..7★: the stars are kept. At ≥ 8★: the pet drops to 0★ and loses the `starGrowAdd` share, unless a Chúc Phúc (`prop_type` 6) is in the safe slot; then one Chúc Phúc is used and the stars are kept.
  - The star item is always consumed. A bound nội đơn needs a bound pet. Confirm text `PETFUNCPANEL_S[55]`.
  - Reply `{flag, petId, starNum, growRateAdd, starSlotId, num, starSafeNum}`. The top-level `growRateAdd` is the client value, i.e. minus the milestone display bonus (pet-system "growRateAdd audit").
  - Stars 9..12 broadcast `CALLBACK_S[30]` "Chúc mừng {name} đã thăng {petName} thành công lên {starNum} sao".
- **Tẩy sao `petStarClear(petId, itemId)`**: Nội Đơn Phụng Hoàng (2245, `prop_type` 5). Sets 0★ and removes `starGrowAdd`. Reply `{flag, msg, petId, starNum:0, growRateAdd, starSlotId, num}`.
- **Dung hợp `petJoin(main, s1, s2, s3, s4)`** (`join.go`):
  - All five pets owned, same `tid`, same colour; orange cannot fuse. Sub pets wear no gear (`PETFUNCPANEL_S[56]` "Phải gỡ trang bị của pet phụ trước khi dung hợp").
  - Rate `PET_JOIN_SUCCESS [100,80,50,30]` by colour. VIP right 6 (`data_tbl_pm_right`) adds its value for purple mains.
  - Success re-rolls `growRate` in the next band with 0.01 steps: green 1.20–1.30, blue 1.31–1.60, purple 1.61–2.00, orange 2.01–2.50. Orange broadcasts a world note.
  - Failure keeps the main pet. The materials are always deleted (`onDelPet` each). A bound material binds the main pet (`onUpdatePet(pid,"binded",1)`).
  - Reply `{flag, pid, grow}`.
- **Dung hợp cao cấp `seniorPetJoin([main, ...subs])`** (`senior_join.go`; also accept `(main, [subs])`):
  - Main colour blue (2) or purple (3). 4..10 (blue) or 4..16 (purple) subs: same tid and colour, no gear, no Mệnh Hồn, not busy.
  - Nội Đơn Tinh Linh 3014: 1 in total for blue, 2 per sub for purple.
  - Rate `ADVANCED_JOIN_PROBABILITY`: blue 50/53/58/65/74/85/100 for 4..10 subs; purple 30/33/36/40/44/49/54/60/66/73/81/90/100 for 4..16 (GamePredef:9915-9937).
  - Result rules as `petJoin`. Refusals answer null + a note.
- **Tẩy Luyện `petXsd(petId, itemId)`** (`xsd.go`):
  - Bound pet; Tẩy Tủy Đan `prop_type` 2, where `propl_num` 1..5 is the aptitude (Lực/Xảo/Nhẫn/Trí/Thần).
  - Normal pills 275-279 roll uniformly in [tpl×0.8, tpl×1.2]; the roll is applied **only if higher** (2026-10-31 rule).
  - Cao Cấp 1652-1656 roll in [cur+1, tpl×1.2] and are refused at the max.
  - The pill is consumed per roll. Reply `{p, t:"aptX", num(stored), i, n, f:true, type, before, after(roll), aptHigh:false}`.
  - An applied roll marks `aptRefined[type]`, and the sheet uses the stored aptitude for that type.
- **Phục Sinh `petPzXsd(petId, itemId)`** (`rebirth.go`):
  - Phẩm Chất Hồi Đơn 1870 (`prop_type` 4). Bound pet, green or better, not at its band max.
  - Re-rolls `growRate` in the current band and keeps only a higher roll.
  - Reply `{p, t:"growRate", num(stored), i, n, f:true, before, after(rolled)}`.
- **Tẩy Điểm `petXd(petId, itemId)`** (`point_reset.go`):
  - Bound thần thú only (creature `class_ids` ∋ 10; `PETFUNCPANEL_S[35]` "Pet tẩy điểm phải là thần thú"). Needs Quy Nguyên Đơn 1526 (`prop_type` 1).
  - Every distributed point goes back to `attrPoints`. Refused when nothing is distributed.
  - Reply `{f, pet:{attStrength…attEnergy, attLastPoint}}`.
- **Ảo Hóa `petEat(main, cost, luckItemId|null, luckNum, aptType, md5)` + `ensurePetEat(main)`** (`illusion.go`):
  - Conditions: main bound, both pets purple or better, the sub pet not busy.
  - Rate per sub pet: 301 → 0.15, 302 → 0.10, 401 → 0.30, 402 → 0.15 (colour×100 + 1 for thần thú, 2 for other). Same race `classId` +0.20, else the rate is halved.
  - +20 % per Đá May Mắn (max 5; Tím 2325 only for a purple sub, Cam 2326 for both). Cap 100 %.
  - The sub pet and the stones are consumed either way.
  - Success rolls `subBaseApt(+Vũ Hóa apt) × 10–15 %` (purple sub) or 25–30 % (orange sub) into a pending value. Reply `{msg, costPet, costItem, isHint:true, petId, aptType, value}`.
  - `ensurePetEat` applies it to `aptIllusion[type]` (replacing the old one) → `{succ:true, propName:"aptXEx", value, msg}`.
  - The md5 (secondary password) is ignored in H5, like `delPetByClient`.
- **Nguyên Tố `elementPet(petId, element)`** (`element.go`):
  - Element 1..6, not the current one. Cost `round(BASIC_GET_MONEY[level] × 0.1)` silver; selected money first, then the other kind.
  - Reply `{petId, element}` + `onAddMoney`. Combat reads `element`.

### 2.3 Tiến hóa, Vũ hóa, Khế ước, Sách Tiến Hóa (12.2)
- **Handbook `activePet({num, pid})` / `activePetGold({num, pid, moneyType:"gold"})`** (`application/pethandbook`):
  - `pid` = `creature_handbook.id`. Activation spends `npPnt` (Năng Lượng Tự Nhiên, currency 236) toward `active_num1`.
  - The gold path is VIP-only and costs `ceil(remaining/5)` gold.
  - State `active_pet` `{pid: {pid, actived, activedNum}}`, sent at login as `activePetObject`. Reply `{pid, actived, activedNum}`, then push `updateActivatePetObj(fullState)`. **Never push a pet through this callback.**
  - Bonus, battle-state pet only: each activated entry adds `prop_type`/`prop_num` (percent when `percent_flag` = 1). A fully activated series (`class_id` 1..8) adds `PET_SERIE_PROP[class]` (GamePredef:10275).
- **Tiến hóa `toPetEnvolution(petId)`** (`evolution.go`):
  - Gates: bound pet, creature `class_ids` ∋ 10, a handbook row with `relate_id = tid` and `evolution_id > 0` that is activated, not evolved yet.
  - Effect: `tid = evolution_id`; name, skills, aptitudes, stars and level are kept; `envoInfo = {envo:true, round:0, stage:0}`. No cost.
  - Reply `{pid, tid, envo:{envo, round, stage}}`, then `onRefreshPetProp`. A following pet is re-created on the map (`onCancelPet` + `onCreatePet`).
- **Vũ Hóa `toPetFeather(petId, useProtect)` / `toPetFeatherNextStep(petId)`** (`feather.go`, `evolution_table.go:46`):
  - `round` 0..2 (Cảnh Giới), `stage` 0..9; round 2 stage 9 is the cap.
  - One try costs `npPnt` `5 × PET_ENVOLUTION_FEATHER_COST[round][stage]` (GamePredef:3237). Success rate `featherSuccessRate[round][stage]`:
    - round 0: 35/29/24/30/24/20/25/20/20;
    - round 1: 29/24/23/24/21/18/20/18/16;
    - round 2: the third row of `evolution_table.go:46` (the implementer copies it).
  - Success: stage+1; stage 9 of rounds 0/1 becomes stage 0 of the next round.
  - Failure: the stage drops to the milestone `floor(stage/3)×3` unless `useProtect` on a non-milestone stage consumes `PET_ENVOLUTION_FEATHER_COST_PROTECT[round][stage]` Bùa Cầu Nguyện (3911). Cảnh Giới never drops.
  - `toPetFeatherNextStep` ("Tăng 1 điểm") pays `PET_ENVOLUTION_FEATHER_COST_GOLD[round][stage]` gold and jumps to stage `(floor(stage/3)+1)×3`.
  - Replies `onFeather {num:1, pid, success, round, stage, protect}` and `OnFeatherNextStep {num:100, …}`.
  - Aptitudes: `aptXEvolution = PET_ENVOLUTION_FEATHER_STEP[tid][attr][round][stage]` (attr 0 Lực, 1 Nhẫn, 2 Xảo, 3 Trí, 4 Thần), added to the sheet aptitude.
  - The table covers 35 evolved creatures (GamePredef:10473+) plus 2332 Kim Lan (= 2293 with Lực and Trí swapped).
  - Strings: `PET_EVOLUTION_PANEL_U[16]` "Không đủ Năng Lượng Tự Nhiên", `[18]` "Vũ Hóa thành công, tăng đến Cảnh Giới num1 cấp num2!", `[19]` "Vũ Hóa thất bại, giảm xuống Cảnh Giới num1 cấp num2".
- **Khế ước `contractPet(type, autoBuy)` / `contractPetTen(type, autoBuy)` / `ensureBuyContractPet(type)`** (`application/petcontract/service.go:1-18`):
  - Types 1 hp, 2 speed, 3 physics, 4 magic (`CONTRACT_DICT`, GamePredef:7039). Max level 50. State `contract_pet {hp, hpExp, speed, speedExp, physics, physicsExp, magic, magicExp}`, sent at login as `contractPet`.
  - One attempt costs `pet_contract[level+1].req_num` Đá Khế Ước (4979) and gives +20 exp (×3 at 5 %, ×2 at 15 %). The contract levels up while exp ≥ `req_exp`, carrying the remainder.
  - Short of stones: without autoBuy the note is "Không đủ Đá Khế Ước". With autoBuy, push `onEnsureBuyContract(missing × 10)` (`PET_EVOLUTION_PANEL_U[62]` "Để tăng cần {money} vàng, xác nhận tiếp tục?"); `ensureBuyContractPet` then buys and runs.
  - `contractPetTen`: up to 10 attempts; it stops at level 50, when stones run out, or when gold runs out.
  - Replies `onContractPet` / `onContractPetTen {type, newLvl, newExp}`.
  - Bonus: `pet_contract[level]` `propN`/`prop_numN` (+`extra_numN` for a Thợ Săn, class 5) as a % (1 HP, 11 speed, 4 atk, 5 matk). It applies to **evolved pets in battle state** only, not in the arena.
  - Assumed values (Go, no source): 10 gold per missing stone; 15 % / 5 % double / triple exp.

### 2.4 Chiến Thú Hộ Vệ (12.3a, `pet_guard.go`, `pet_guard_upgrade.go`, `pet_guard_bonus.go`)
- **State** `pet_guard {lvData:{"0".."4"}, petData:{"0".."4","11".."45"}}`. Levels are 1-based: centre 1..5, guards 1..30.
  - Sub-pet keys are `sid*10 + 1..5`. A stored 0 is healed to 1.
  - Sent at login as `petGuardData`; the tokens `petguardout` (200) and `petguardin` (201) are sent too.
- **`movePetToPetGuardSid(petId, sid)`**: thần thú (`class_ids` ∋ 10) with `use_lv ≥ 50` (`PANEL_PETGUARD[10]`), no duplicates. `putDownPet(sid)` clears the slot.
- **`upGuardSid(sid, costType)`**: costType 2 = tokens (default), 1 = gold after the confirm "Không đủ Thủ Phù, dùng {num} Vàng".
  - Centre cost by current level: 1: 75/7500, 2: 150/15000, 3: 250/25000, 4: 380/38000 (num/gold; Flash `PetGuardPanel.as:1029` `PET_GUARD_ADD`).
  - Guards: `pet_guard` row (lev = current, sid) `num`/`gold`.
  - Max 5 / 30. Notes: "Không đủ <currency> để cường hóa <slot> (cần N, có M)." / "<slot> đã đạt cấp tối đa!".
- **Bonus**: the centre pet (slot 0) and the four Chiến Thú pets (slots 1..4) only. Hộ Vệ sub-pets give nothing themselves; they raise the multipliers.
  - Centre: for each guard 1..4, row (lv, sid), prop = `propVal/10000 × (1+inner/100) × (1+centre/100) × (1+guard/100)`, where:
    - `inner` = Σ `[0.0016,0.08,0.4,2,10][color]` over the 5 sub-pets;
    - `centre` = `{1:0,2:3,3:8,4:15,5:25}[lv0]`;
    - `guard` = guard pet colour + 1.
  - Chiến Thú pet in sid: `propVal/10000 × (1+inner/100)`.
  - Level-1 rows have propVal 0. Units: 59/60/62/63 ×100 into percent rates.
  - Applies to the panel, battle and the arena.

### 2.5 Tụ Linh Bảo Thạch (12.3b, `application/petstone`)
- **Stones** are `pet_stone` records (22 families × 6 levels; level = colour; level 6 = Tụ Linh Thạch, `energy_flag` 1), not items.
  - The bag is the feature state `pet_stone {b:{slot:{g, n, s}}}`, sent as a sparse array `[tid, num, skillId]`.
- **Dung Hợp `composePetStone(sid, qty, cid)`**:
  - 3 identical stones → 1 `next_id`, qty times. Source level < 5 and not energy (`PET_STONE_PANEL[8]` "Thú thạch cấp 5 hoặc Tụ Linh Thạch không thể dung hợp").
  - Cost `cost_sil × qty` silver (selected kind first, never split). Everything is checked before anything is charged.
- **Tụ Linh `absorbPetStone([main, s1..s4], cid)`**: five level-5 regular stones → the main stone's `energy_id`. No cost.
- **Khảm**:
  - `getPetStoneByEid(itemId)` → `{hole:[tid,0,skillId]}`, `{}` when there is no stone, `-1` only for a refused item.
  - `petStoneSet(itemId, hole, sid, cid)` / `removePetStone(itemId, hole, cid, itemId)` → `{bagData, slotId, newId, skillId, equData}`.
  - Rules: kind-9 gear owned by the player; hole 1 takes only a Tụ Linh Thạch and vice versa; one stone per `prop_type` per item; an occupied hole is refused (`PET_STONE_PANEL[47]` "Lỗ này đã có bảo thạch, phải gỡ mới có thể khảm vào").
  - Holes are stored on the item (`flag3` JSON `{"1":[tid,0,skillId]}`).
- **Luyện `changePetStoneEnergy(sid, cid, type)`**: an energy stone in the bag; costs 500 / 1000 `energyStone` (passive / special skill). Uniform draw from the ex-skill pool. Reply `{sid, skillId, bagData, newSid}`.
- **Phân Giải `resolvePetStone(sid, num, cid)`**: `resolve_num × num` `energyStone` (currency 235).
- **Bag push** `updatePetStoneBagData(bag)` after grants. Legacy gap: inlaid stones gave **no stats** (see Decisions).

### 2.6 Thiên Phú Pet (12.3c, `application/pettalent`)
- **State** `pet_talent {tal:{sid:tid}, inTal:{sid:tid}, b:{slot:{t,n}}}`.
  - `sid = page×10000 + slot`: slots 1..9 are nodes, 10..11 are stone sockets. The bag has 320 slots.
  - Character-wide: the bonus applies to **every pet**.
- **`upTalentSlotLv(sid, useGold)`**: the next record is (sid, lv+1, `exp = 0`). Cost `up_exp` `pvePoint` (Anh Linh, currency 238) or `ceil(up_exp/15)` gold.
  - Socket 10 needs slots 1..4 to sum ≥ 20; socket 11 needs slots 1..9 ≥ 35 (Flash `PetTalentPanel.as:2651` `checkSpecSlotUp`).
- **`fillTalentStone(bagSlot, socketSid, tid)` / `takeOffTalentStone(socketSid, bagSlot, tid)`**:
  - Gates: the socket is open (`TALENT_PANEL_FUNC_U[20]` "Ô Thiên Phú chưa kích hoạt, không thể khảm nạm"); stone lv ≤ socket lv (`[21]`); stone quality ≤ socket (`[22]`).
  - Push `onFillOrTakeOffTalentStone({bag:{a,d}, pan:{a:{sid:{tid}}, d:{sid:1}}})`.
- **Bag operations**:
  - `upTalentStone("s1|..", "t1|..", useDirectionItem)`: 5 same-level stones → lv+1 with a uniform line draw. With the direction item 4181 ×{1:1,2:3,3:15,4:50}[lv], the same line is kept.
  - `resetTalentSlot`: 2 same-level stones → 1 re-rolled stone.
  - `breakTalentStone(slot, tid, n)`: `n × exp` `pvePoint`.
  - `arrangeTalentBag`, `moveStoneBagToBag`.
- **Bonus**: Σ `prop_val` per `prop_type` over `tal` + `inTal`. Stone power = Σ `p` of `inTal`; rate 10/8/6/4/2 % at power ≥ 850/700/400/200/100.
  - Final value = `floor(sum × (1+rate/100)) / 100`. Additive.
  - Mapping: 1/2/4..11/13 flat; 31/32/59/60/61/62/63 rates; 58 skipped.

### 2.7 Mệnh Hồn soul gems (12.3d, `application/soul`)
- **Rows**: `pet_soul` has one row per (family, level); a family is consecutive ids with the same name and colour. Row `exp` = base dissolve value, `up_exp` = cost to the next level.
- **Containers**:
  - Character bag: 16 slots, 12 free (`openBag` opens 13..16 at 10 gold each).
  - Temp prey bag: 14 slots.
  - Pet ring: 8 slots, opened by pet level `PET_SOUL_LEVEL [80,80,80,80,80,100,120,140]`. `openNum2` adds extra slots through `openPetSoulBag`, paid with soul exp, needs openNum ≥ 5.
- **Currencies**: `soulExp` (Exp Mệnh Hồn), `soulChip` (Mảnh Vỡ, 242), `soulPnt` (Pha Lê). Never mix them.
- **Xủ Quẻ `preySoul(index, quick, moneyFlag)`**:
  - Crystal N draws uniformly from `type = -1`, level-1 rows with `color = N-1`.
  - Crystal N lights N+1 at 50/40/30/20 % (Go assumption), else falls back to crystal 1.
  - Pha Lê price per crystal from the Flash `SOUL_CRSTAL_COST`.
  - Advanced (`moneyFlag`, VIP): 200 gold; the 7 special oranges at 0.1 % each, else uniform over the purple and orange classic families.
  - Failures push `onPreySoulSingle {type}`: 2 = "Không đủ pha lê", 1 = "Không thể xủ quẻ".
- **`soulLevelUp(index[, petId])`**: spends `up_exp - exp` from `soulExp`, moves to sid+1, max level 10. Pet ring slots are sent as `slot + 100`.
- **Dissolve** `transformExp(slot|maxColor, bulk)`: refund = base `exp` + Σ `up_exp` of the lower levels + in-level exp, plus `chip`. Bulk is VIP only and covers unlocked gems with colour ≤ maxColor-1.
- **`moveSoul(sid, newSid, petId|-1)`**: a pet ring refuses a second gem of the same kind (`onUpdateSoulSlot {f:0}`, "Mệnh Hồn cùng loại không thể xuất hiện cùng lúc").
- **`exchangeSoul`** buys classic purple/orange gems for `req_chip` Mảnh Vỡ.
- **Pet ring bonus**: `prop_val` of the gem's current row (display units; 61 is stored ×100).
  - Flats 1/4..11/13/34; rates 31/32/59..63; status ids 43..55/70 → the matching resist / hit rates.
- **Push** `onPetLevelUp({openNum, pid})` (one object) when a level-up opens a ring slot.

### 2.8 Pet gear upgrades (12.4, `application/item/pet_equip_*.go`, `sublime*.go`)
- **Thăng cấp `petEquLevelUp(equipId, shardItemId)`**:
  - Same item id; `templateId` becomes `nextEquTid`. Always succeeds.
  - Shards 2333 × `LEVELUP_REQ_NUM[reqLevel].req`: 40:5, 60:10, 80:15, 100:20, 120:25, 140:50, 160:50.
  - Positions 56-57 use `SPEC_LEVELUP_REQ_NUM` (item 2529: 5/10/15/20/25/30; GamePredef:1790-1845).
  - Silver `reqLevel² × 10` of the next template. Durability max is set to the new tier's max.
  - Reply `onPetEquLevelUp({flag, i, tid, sid, next, num, msg})`.
- **Đổi màu `petEquModColor(equipId, shardItemId)`**:
  - Display colour 0..3 → +1. `MODCOLOR_REQ_NUM`: 0 → 2334×1 at 80 %, 1 → ×2 at 50 %, 2 → ×4 at 30 %, 3 → 2335×8 at 20 %. Spec gear: 2528 ×1/2/4/6.

- **Đổi khóa and Tái thiết lập**: both need bound gear and 30000 silver, and the material is spent at the preview.

- **Thăng hoa and Đổi khắc chế**:
  - `sublimePetEquip(equipId, dustSlotId|NaN, autoBuy)` → `onSublimePetEquip({itemId, flagStr, num})`.

- **Repair** `repairPetEqu(petId, itemId, type)` / `repairPetEquAll(petId, type)`: 10 silver per point, like `repair`. Pet gear never wears down in Go, so this is parity only.

## 3. Decisions for H5

1. **Pet stat providers**: add a pet bonus registry, `PET_STAT_BONUS_PROVIDERS`, beside the character one.
   - Each provider says in which contexts it applies: panel, battle, arena, ppve.
   - It replaces the ad-hoc gear and passive merge in `pet-bonus.service.ts`; the parity test pins today's numbers.
2. **The client is ours.**
   - Where the Flash client used a null Responder and an unknown push (guard, talent fill), H5 RPCs also **return** the new state.
   - The legacy push is still sent when its name is known.
   - Payload field names stay legacy.
3. **Stats for H5-only gaps**:
   - Inlaid pet stones give **no** stats, as in Go (user decision 2026-11-04: follow the original). The hole `prop_type` / `prop_num` are stored and shown only.
   - The Tụ Linh hole skill is stored and shown, but not cast (§8).
4. **Rates**: PetProperty gains an optional `rates` map (Go final* rate keys). Combat consumes only the rate keys `combat/damage.ts` already reads; the others are display-only until combat grows (risk 2).
5. **Secondary password** args (md5) are accepted and ignored, as in M4.
6. **Assumed constants** (Go, no source) are kept verbatim and listed in one `pet-depth-constants.ts` per module, so they are easy to tune:
   - contract: 10 gold per stone, 15 % / 5 % double / triple exp;
   - crystal advance 50/40/30/20 %;
   - fuse uniform line;
   - PPVE scaling;
   - arena points.
7. **Spec gear tables**: positions 56-57 use the Flash `SPEC_LEVELUP_REQ_NUM` / `SPEC_MODCOLOR_REQ_NUM` (items 2529 / 2528), not the Go ×1.5 / ×2 multiplier. The diff is recorded in the memory doc.
8. **Simulated pet battles**: one server-only `PetSimBattle` runner on the existing `round.ts` engine. No client commands; scripted per round.
   - It produces the same `onBattleStart`-shaped start and play-list groups the client already renders. A replay opens in watch mode on `BattleStage`.
   - Replays are kept in memory, the last 20 per character. Single process.
9. **Transfer locks**: trade refuses a pet that is in a guard slot, in an arena / PPVE line-up, evolved-pending or wearing gear. Extend the M5 trade pet gate.
10. **Monetization**: every gold path that has data or a Flash price is included:
    - `activePetGold`, contract autoBuy, feather gold jump, guard gold;
    - talent gold, advanced Xủ Quẻ, arena turns / cd, PPVE gold;
    - sublime autoBuy.
    VIP gates read the existing VIP service. Top-up stays "not ready".
11. **Announcements** use the existing system-channel broadcast (`scenes.broadcastAll` `onSystemSay`).
12. **Owner level cap adopted** (user decision 2026-11-04): Phase A ports the Go rule (pet ≤ owner level + 5, max 160,
    exp clamped to the start of the cap level, Flash "Pet đã vượt quá bạn 5 cấp, không thể tiếp tục nhận kinh nghiệm!")
    and updates the M4 pet exp paths (`pet/pet-level.ts`, combat settle, quest award). Pets already above the cap keep
    their level and stop gaining exp until the owner catches up; no rewrite script.
13. **Currencies without an H5 source** (user decision 2026-11-04): follow the original rules and costs. Sources that
    H5 does not have yet (guard tokens, Năng Lượng Tự Nhiên, Anh Linh, Pha Lê, Đá Khế Ước, …) stay unwired for now; the
    features ship spendable, tested with GM `/grant`, and the sources land with the milestones that own them.
14. **Arena daily turns**: 20, as the Go server; the Flash "10 cơ hội" text is display only and is flagged in memory.

## 10. Risks and open questions

**Risks**
1. **Stat drift**: moving gear and passive into the registry must not change today's numbers (parity test). The aptitude sum changes stats only for pets with refined / evolved / illusion values.
2. **Rate stats**: many boosters grant rate props (59-63, 31, 32, resists). If H5 combat does not read a rate key, the bonus is display-only. The implementer lists which keys `combat/damage.ts` uses and records the gaps in memory.
3. **Simulator effort**: Phases F and G need a non-interactive battle runner and a replay viewer. If `round.ts` is too coupled to sessions, F grows. Prototype first.
4. **Character-wide refresh fan-out**: talent, guard and handbook changes re-enrich every pet. Push only to the battle pet and the slot pets, and recompute lazily on panel open.
5. **Invented constants** (§3.6) may differ from the original game.
6. **Size**: about 75 RPCs. Each phase has 8-20; keep the rpc files thin and split by tab.
